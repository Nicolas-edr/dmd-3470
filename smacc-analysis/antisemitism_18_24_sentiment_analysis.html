Key findings for the 18–24 demographic

TikTok is the primary vector. 3,035 mentions with a heavily positive sentiment ratio (29% positive vs 20% negative) — content is framed sympathetically, not as overt hate, making it less likely to trigger moderation or viewer skepticism.
Micro-accounts, macro-reach. Account ._af10x has only 397 followers yet achieved 1.9M reach. This is the hallmark of algorithmic amplification — small accounts using #fyp and #viral to reach enormous undirected audiences.
Geopolitical framing as entry point. Hashtags like #freepalestine (2,946), #gaza, and #iran are used as the entry framing, meaning users discover content through political solidarity channels — not explicit searches for antisemitic content.
Institutional amplifiers present. CNN and Sky News TikTok accounts appear in the influencer table, lending mainstream legitimacy to adjacent content in the same discovery feed.
X has the sharpest negative skew. 77% of X (Twitter) mentions are negative — this platform functions more as a reactive/critical space, while TikTok functions as a sympathetic one. Different platforms, different emotional register for the same underlying content.

How content reaches the 18–24 cohort — pathway analysis

Step 1 — Entry via solidarity
User engages with #freepalestine or #gaza content — framed as humanitarian or political solidarity, not ideological.
Step 2 — Algorithmic pull
TikTok/Bluesky algorithms serve adjacent content. #fyp and #viral tags are embedded to maximize this distribution to undirected viewers.
Step 3 — Normalization loop
Consistent exposure (17K mentions / 2 weeks, 75M reach) normalizes the framing. TikTok's positive sentiment ratio (29%) suggests the content is not perceived as hostile.
Step 4 — Cross-platform spread
Content migrates to Bluesky (2,709), Reddit (496), and Telegram (82) — including more closed/ideological spaces. Telegram links suggest some content flows into encrypted/private channels.

Critical data limitations for this analysis

Brand24 does not provide direct age demographic segmentation. The 18–24 inference is based on platform demographics (TikTok's user base skews heavily 18–29) and account characteristics of top influencers, not direct age data.
The dataset tracks the keyword "antisemitism" — content that promotes antisemitism without using the term itself (e.g. coded language, dog-whistles) would not appear in this data. True exposure is likely higher.
Instagram and Facebook show 0 mentions — this is almost certainly a data access limitation (Meta API restrictions), not true zero presence.
